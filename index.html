Carbon Emission Intelligence Platform

A full-stack platform for tracking and reporting carbon emissions, with a RAG layer that lets users ask questions about their own data.

In active development

Scope 1 and 2 emissions work is under way. Scope 3 and the commercial side are not implemented yet and are documented as planned. Every doc marks what is built, in progress and planned, so you can see the real state of the project.

What I designed and built

  • Next.js frontend and FastAPI backend, containerised with Docker.
  • A dual-database data layer: 10+ relational tables in SQL Server for users, projects and companies, and 12+ MongoDB collections for document data.
  • A RAG and vector-embeddings layer for querying and reporting in plain language.
  • Schema validation and standardised preprocessing so structured and unstructured data stay consistent.
